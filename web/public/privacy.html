Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Spoonfull ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Spoonfull POS client application. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information in connection with our point-of-sale services.

1. Information We Collect

Spoonfull POS is a merchant tool designed to manage and print restaurant orders. We collect and process the following information to perform services:

  • Account credentials: Email and password for restaurant/merchant admin authentication.
  • Order details: Customer names, delivery addresses, phone numbers, item details, order notes, and transaction values required to print receipts and coordinate delivery.
  • Device metadata: Hardware statuses (e.g., printer paper alerts) to optimize thermal printing output.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to facilitate kitchen order workflows, print receipts, and synchronize storefront statuses. This includes:

  • Generating physical and digital order receipts.
  • Assigning deliveries to drivers.
  • Updating Hyperzod catalog items and storefront open/closed statuses.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or operational data with third parties. Data is shared only with Supabase (our backend database partner) and Hyperzod (our primary e-commerce system provider) for service operations.

4. Security

We implement strict administrative and technical security measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and row-level security (RLS) on database tables.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@spoonfull.nl.
